plan3d privacy policy

plan3d is a floor-plan editor that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server and no accounts.

Your plans

Plans are stored in your browser (localStorage) and in files you export. The developer does not receive them.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose "Save to Google Drive" or "Load from Google Drive", you sign in with Google and allow the app to create and read its own file, plan3d.json, in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). The app cannot see any other file in your Drive. The file is sent directly between your browser and Google. The sign-in token is kept in memory only and is gone when you close the page.

Data collected

None. No analytics, no cookies set by the app, nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties other than Google as described above.
